About

Curiosity, with a method.

I’m interested in how data and technology can help answer practical questions, especially in public health and education. My work has included research collaboration, statistical analysis, and building digital tools that make information easier to find and use.

I enjoy moving between the details of a dataset and the bigger question behind it: what can we reasonably conclude, what remains uncertain, and how could the result be useful?

I value transparent methods, careful interpretation, and software that is useful to people beyond the team that built it.

Approach

From question to result.

01 / FRAME

Define the question

Clarify what the data can answer and what a useful result would look like.

02 / CHECK

Understand the evidence

Inspect data quality, assumptions, limitations, and sources before interpreting patterns.

03 / ANALYZE

Use appropriate methods

Apply statistical and computational methods suited to the question, not just the methods that are easiest to run.

04 / SHARE

Make the work useful

Communicate findings clearly and build tools or documentation that help other people use them.
