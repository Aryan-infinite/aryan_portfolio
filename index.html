Flagship · First-author preprint

Does Group Fairness Guarantee Recourse Fairness?

Research question. Fairness metrics usually assess how a model treats groups. This study asks whether a fairness intervention also makes the path to a different outcome equally reachable for people the model rejects.

Data and approach. I examine the Adult Census Income and COMPAS decision domains using linear classifiers. The experiments compare a baseline with Reweighing and covariance-penalized logistic regression, then assess group-fairness measures alongside the geometric distance from a rejected individual's case to the model's decision boundary.

My work. I led the study's conceptualization, methodology, implementation, validation, and analysis. The evaluation uses 30 randomized data splits, with repeated model fitting and penalty sweeps to test the stability of the analysis. This distance is a mathematical measure under the model, not a claim that every feature can be changed in real life.

First author · Paper under peer review

Google Trends and dengue surveillance in Nepal (2019–2024)

Research question. Could online search behaviour provide a timely signal that complements routine dengue surveillance in Nepal?

Data and approach. The study brings together 72 monthly observations from 2019–2024, Google Trends activity for three dengue-related search terms, and 161,892 laboratory-confirmed cases. It examines contemporaneous associations and time-lag patterns using correlation and regression methods, asking whether changes in search interest track changes in reported disease activity.

My work. I led the study and its data analysis, including assembling the monthly series, comparing search terms, and examining the relationship between search activity and confirmed cases. The paper is under peer review.

Machine learning · Co-author

E. coli ciprofloxacin resistance

Research question. Do genome-based models for predicting ciprofloxacin resistance remain reliable when evaluated on bacterial populations from different geographic settings?

Data and approach. This collaborative study develops a genome-based prediction workflow and evaluates transfer across geographically distinct E. coli datasets. It considers both predictive discrimination and the reliability of predicted probabilities, using external validation and post-hoc recalibration to examine model confidence beyond the training population.

My contribution. I contributed to software, formal analysis, and model validation, and assisted with reviewing and editing the manuscript.

Survey research · Bhairahawa

Caffeine use among 204 adults

Research question. How do adults in Bhairahawa consume caffeinated drinks, and what patterns of dependence or withdrawal do they report?

Data and approach. The survey collected responses from 204 adults in Siddharthanagar-09, Bhairahawa. It examined consumption frequency and drink preferences, timing of use including consumption on an empty stomach, and self-reported dependence and withdrawal experiences. Descriptive statistics and group comparisons were used to characterize the responses.

My work. I contributed to the study's statistical analysis and interpretation of the survey data.

Leadership Skill Development Training (LSDT)

How it beganI co-founded LSDT in Bhairahawa after a grade-nine public-speaking experience showed me how few accessible spaces local students had to practise speaking and leadership. With a mentor, I helped grow a weekly gathering of around 15 participants into a free community learning program.

What it becameLSDT brings together students, parents, teachers, and working professionals for weekly sessions on public speaking, storytelling, communication, teamwork, life skills, and practical opportunities.

My contributionAs co-founder and former administrator, I helped build the weekly operating structure, coordinate the admin team and guest speakers, and develop the youth chapter. I taught basic computer skills to around 300 members and public-speaking skills to more than 1,000 participants. I also helped connect students with opportunities, including two full scholarships to Horizon G.B.S. through Attotech, and built a handoff structure for the youth team to continue running programs independently.
