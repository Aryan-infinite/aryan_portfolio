04 / About

Researcher by question. Builder by habit.

I am Aryan Bhusal, from Bhairahawa, Nepal. I work independently and with collaborators on questions in algorithmic fairness, public-health data, and machine learning. Alongside research, I build data pipelines and digital resources that make information easier to use. I am interested in the point where careful analysis meets a practical problem: who is affected, what evidence is available, and what can be built to help?

I want the work to be useful beyond the paper or the screen.
